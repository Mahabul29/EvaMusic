{{ _("Privacy Policy") }}

This policy explains what information EvaMusic collects and how it is used. By using EvaMusic, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

If you create an account, we store your username and password (encrypted) to let you log in and sync your profile. If you don't create an account, EvaMusic works as a guest and most of your preferences (theme, accent color, streaming server, etc.) are saved only on your own device.

How We Use Your Information

Account details are used solely to manage your login session and profile. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Music Content

Songs streamed through EvaMusic are retrieved from third-party music catalogs and streaming servers. EvaMusic does not host or store the audio files itself; it acts as a player and search interface for content available through those external sources.

Local Storage & Cookies

EvaMusic uses your browser's local storage to remember settings such as theme, accent color, preferred server, and playback preferences. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless required for account login.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect any account information you provide, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Please use a unique password for your EvaMusic account.

Children's Privacy

EvaMusic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally as EvaMusic evolves. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us
