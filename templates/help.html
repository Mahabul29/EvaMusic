Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create an account?
Go to Settings and tap "Sign Up" under the Account section. Pick a username (at least 3 characters) and a password (at least 6 characters), then you're all set.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
Currently there's no automated password reset. Please contact support below with your username and we'll help you regain access.
How do I change my password?
Log in, then go to Settings → Security → Change Password. You'll need to enter your current password followed by your new one.
Will I lose my favorites if I sign up later?
No. Any songs you've favorited as a guest are automatically carried over to your new account when you sign up.
A song won't play or shows an error.
Streaming links can occasionally expire or fail to load. Try going back and reopening the song, or switch your Music Server in Settings (HD1 / HD2) and try again.
How do I free up storage used by the app?
Go to Settings → Data & Storage → Clear Cache. This removes temporarily cached files without affecting your favorites, playlists, or account.
